# 1. Purpose

The product backlog contains multiple potentially valuable capabilities.

However, building every capability simultaneously would increase implementation effort, operational risk, and time to validate the product hypothesis.

This document prioritizes features using the **RICE framework**.

The objective is to identify which capabilities should be included in the MVP and which should be deferred.

---

# 2. Prioritization Framework

RICE evaluates each feature using four dimensions:

### Reach

Estimated proportion of support workflows affected by the capability.

Scale:

- 1 = Very limited reach
- 2 = Limited reach
- 3 = Moderate reach
- 4 = High reach
- 5 = Very high reach

### Impact

Potential effect on the identified business problems.

Scale:

- 0.5 = Minimal
- 1 = Low
- 2 = Moderate
- 3 = High
- 4 = Very high

### Confidence

How strongly the available evidence supports the expected value.

Scale:

- 50% = Low confidence
- 70% = Medium confidence
- 85% = High confidence
- 100% = Very high confidence

### Effort

Relative product, engineering, data, and testing effort.

Scale:

- 1 = Very low
- 2 = Low
- 3 = Moderate
- 4 = High
- 5 = Very high

---
# 3. RICE Formula
**RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort**
