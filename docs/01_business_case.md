# Business Case — AI Customer Support & Resolution Assistant

## 1. Project Overview

**Project:** AI Customer Support & Resolution Assistant  
**Company:** ServeFlow (Fictional)  
**Role:** Business Analyst & Product Manager  
**Project Type:** B2B SaaS / AI Product  
**Status:** Discovery

---

## 2. Company Background

ServeFlow is a fictional B2B SaaS platform that helps small and mid-sized businesses manage customer orders, subscriptions, payments, and related support operations.

As ServeFlow's customer base grows, the volume and complexity of customer support requests have increased.

Customers contact the support team through multiple channels including email, chat, web forms, and in-app support.

The support organization currently relies heavily on manual processes for ticket classification, prioritization, investigation, and response generation.

---

## 3. Business Context

The growing volume of support requests has created operational challenges for the customer support organization.

Support agents spend significant time manually reviewing customer conversations, determining ticket categories and priorities, searching for relevant information, and writing responses.

Support managers also have limited visibility into the underlying causes of recurring customer issues and the factors contributing to escalations, SLA breaches, and repeat contacts.

The organization therefore needs to evaluate opportunities to improve support efficiency while maintaining human oversight and service quality.

---

## 4. Problem Statement

ServeFlow's customer support team relies heavily on manual ticket triage, investigation, and response creation.

This can result in:

- Longer ticket resolution times
- Inconsistent ticket prioritization
- Repetitive work for support agents
- Unnecessary escalations
- Difficulty identifying recurring customer issues
- Limited visibility into root causes
- Potential SLA breaches
- Inconsistent customer experiences

The business needs a more structured and intelligent support workflow that helps agents resolve customer issues efficiently while giving managers better visibility into support performance and recurring problems.

---

## 5. Business Opportunity

There is an opportunity to introduce an AI-assisted support layer that can help transform unstructured customer requests into structured, actionable information.

Potential capabilities include:

- Automatic ticket classification
- Priority recommendations
- Customer conversation summarization
- Suggested resolutions
- AI-assisted response drafting
- Recurring issue detection
- Root-cause insights
- Support performance analytics

The proposed solution will keep human agents in control of customer-facing decisions.

---

## 6. Initial Product Hypothesis

> If support agents are provided with AI-assisted ticket classification, prioritization, summarization, resolution suggestions, and response drafting, then the support organization may be able to reduce manual effort, improve resolution consistency, and provide managers with better visibility into recurring customer issues.

This hypothesis will be evaluated through data analysis, requirements analysis, product design, and MVP validation.

---

## 7. Project Objectives

### Primary Objectives

1. Understand the major operational challenges within the existing support workflow.
2. Identify the highest-impact opportunities for process improvement.
3. Analyse support-ticket data to identify patterns and drivers of poor performance.
4. Define requirements for an AI-assisted support workflow.
5. Design an MVP focused on the highest-value use cases.
6. Define measurable product and business KPIs.
7. Develop a roadmap for future product capabilities.

### Secondary Objectives

- Improve agent productivity.
- Reduce repetitive manual work.
- Improve ticket prioritization.
- Improve management visibility.
- Identify recurring product/customer issues.
- Establish a human-in-the-loop AI workflow.

---

## 8. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Customer support ticket analysis
- Current-state process analysis
- Stakeholder analysis
- User and business requirements
- AI-assisted ticket classification
- Priority recommendations
- Ticket summarization
- Suggested resolutions
- AI response drafting
- Support analytics dashboard
- Product metrics
- MVP definition
- Product roadmap
- Business impact assessment

### Out of Scope for MVP

- Fully autonomous customer support
- Automatic customer-facing message sending
- Automatic refunds or financial transactions
- Direct modification of customer accounts
- Complete replacement of human support agents
- Real-time integration with every external support platform

---

## 9. Key Assumptions

The initial project assumes that:

- Historical support-ticket data is available for analysis.
- Tickets contain sufficient textual information for classification and analysis.
- A knowledge base containing support documentation exists.
- Support agents remain responsible for final customer-facing decisions.
- AI recommendations can be reviewed before being used.
- Historical performance metrics can be used to establish a baseline.

These assumptions will be validated during the discovery phase.

---

## 10. Initial Success Criteria

The project will initially evaluate whether the proposed solution can contribute to improvements in:

- Average resolution time
- First response time
- SLA breach rate
- Escalation rate
- Repeat contact rate
- Agent handling effort
- Customer satisfaction
- Ticket classification accuracy
- AI recommendation acceptance rate

Specific target values will be defined after analysing the baseline dataset.

---

## 11. Key Business Questions

The discovery and analysis phase will attempt to answer:

1. Which support categories generate the highest ticket volumes?
2. Which categories have the longest resolution times?
3. What factors are associated with SLA breaches?
4. Which ticket types are most frequently escalated?
5. Which issues result in repeat customer contacts?
6. Which support activities involve the most repetitive manual work?
7. Which customer segments generate the highest support burden?
8. Which AI use cases have the greatest potential business impact?
9. Where should human review remain mandatory?
10. How should the success of the MVP be measured?

---

## 12. Initial Risks

| Risk | Potential Impact | Initial Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Poor-quality historical data | Weak analysis and AI performance | Data quality assessment |
| Incorrect AI recommendations | Customer/service risk | Human-in-the-loop review |
| Low agent adoption | Limited business impact | Agent feedback and usability testing |
| Over-automation | Loss of human oversight | Define clear automation boundaries |
| Insufficient training data | Weak classification | Evaluate data coverage |
| AI hallucination | Incorrect responses | Ground responses in approved knowledge |
| Privacy concerns | Compliance and trust issues | Minimize sensitive data exposure |
