# 1. Roadmap Objective

The roadmap translates the prioritized product backlog into a phased delivery plan.

The initial objective is not to build a fully autonomous support system.

The MVP will validate whether AI assistance can improve the support-agent workflow across:

> **Understand → Triage → Investigate → Escalate**

The product will then use pilot evidence to determine which capabilities should be expanded, improved, or automated.
